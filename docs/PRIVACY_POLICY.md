# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 11, 2026**

**SolarPulse by Qashfin** is committed to clear, ethical data privacy.

1. **Zero personal identifiers stored.** We do not collect names, phone numbers, exact residential street addresses or payment card details.
2. **Calculation inputs.** The electricity bill figure, broad city and roof type you enter are processed ephemerally in memory during the request and are never saved to long-term storage or sold to solar sales networks.
3. **Abuse-prevention storage.** An anonymized, salted hash of your IP address is kept for up to 60 minutes in a temporary DynamoDB entry, solely to guard against denial-of-service attempts and rate abuse.
4. **Cloud infrastructure processing.** All calculations and advisory analyses run on Amazon Web Services infrastructure (API Gateway, Lambda, DynamoDB and Amazon Bedrock) following cloud security best practices. Application logs record operational errors and do not include the values you enter.
